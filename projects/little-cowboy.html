The Jam

Little Cowboy was made for the GMTK 2026 Game Jam with the theme of “Countdown”. It is a deck building action roguelike, where the player chooses different bullets to upgrade their revolver with combos and synergies. The revolver only has 6 slots, so the player must make important decisions on what bullets to take, and what order to put them in.

Designing From Abroad

I led the design for this game, but I was abroad in Thailand when the jam took place, meaning I was in the opposite time zone from the rest of my team. This made it very important to have clear documentation and communication.

  • We had meetings every morning and evening when there was an overlap in our waking hours to discuss our progress, have design meetings, and plan out what to do each day.
  • I created a comprehensive task tracking sheet to make sure everyone knew what they needed to get done. If I needed something done I could leave it there and wait for them to wake up and vice versa.

Designing the Bullets

  • I drafted 37 different bullet ideas that could all work with each other.
  • I intentionally made an excessive amount of ideas so that we could pick and choose our favorites to implement with a large backlog to choose from.
  • 10 different bullets made it into the game.

Fire and Oil

  • One of the simpler combos: when fire goes over oil spilled on the ground, it erupts in flames.

Vortex

  • Vortex pulls enemies towards where it hits.
  • It can combo with other bullets by manipulating the position of enemies.
  • Pairs well with explosive bullets, or builds on top of the synergies of other combos like fire and oil.

Designing the Enemies

  • I designed a roster of enemies to challenge the player.
  • Similarly to the bullets, I concepted 21 different enemies with the intention of about ¼ of them being implemented.
  • 6 enemies ended up making it into the game that provide different challenges to the player.

The Tumbleweed

The Tumbleweed is a basic enemy that will roll towards you and chase you down. It will die in one hit, but it is fast, forcing the player to react quickly.

The Dynamite Thrower

The Dynamite Thrower throws sticks of dynamite at the player from afar and can throw over obstacles. He is weak, but challenging to get close enough to kill. However, the player can use the dynamite he throws to their advantage, because it also damages enemies.

Results

Out of all 10,533 games in the jam, Little Cowboy was the 48th most played game. The game was also ranked #565 for Audio and #653 for Enjoyment.
